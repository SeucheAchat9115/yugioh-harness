# Angelechy playbook

## Overview

40 Main / 15 Extra / 15 Side. [YDK](deck.ydk) · [Gameplay data](deck.json) ·
[Event list](README.md). Build a board with Fairy Synchros placed as Continuous
Spells. Choose the order and columns of placements so each search or restriction
has a job. Main Deck monsters supply hand interaction. The engine monsters live
in the Extra Deck.

## Synergies

Bastion in the Spell/Trap Zone starts a second placement and protects other
Angelechy cards from effect destruction. Shatranga adds a Trap and reduces an
opponent's long monster chain of plays. Castellan can limit Chains for both
players, so resolve essential setup before placing it if you need more than one
activation in a Chain. Destrier rewards leaving it placed when opposing effects
accumulate. Reserve free Spell/Trap Zones for upcoming placements.

Enlisted plus a Level 7 non-Tuner makes Kusanagi. Enlisted plus a Level 8
Bastion can make Chaos Angel or Masterflare Hyperion when legal. Enlisted plus
an eligible Level 10 non-Tuner can make Legatia. A placed Synchro cannot be
used as ordinary Synchro Material. Check the relevant Dominus locks before
choosing an effect, even when the summon itself is permitted.

## Combo lines

**Problem plus Verdict.** Start with both in hand, no locks, empty zones, and
Enlisted, Bastion, and Shatranga available in the Extra Deck.

1. Activate Problem, then use its effect and discard Verdict as cost.
2. If it resolves, summon Enlisted and place Bastion. On the next eligible
   Chain, use Bastion to place Shatranga.
3. Use Shatranga's resulting trigger to add Opening from Deck or recover Verdict
   from GY. End with Enlisted, Problem, two placed Synchros and the chosen Trap.
   If you recover Verdict, its GY placement is unavailable until it reaches the GY again.
4. If Problem is stopped, keep the remaining engine cards in Extra. If Verdict
   reached GY, consider its placement effect only with an eligible Angelechy to
   send and a free zone.

**Opening on the opponent's first turn.** Start second with Opening in hand,
an available Extra Monster Zone, and Problem or Endgame Problem available.
Before making a commitment, evaluate Fuwalos or Purulia while you control no cards.

1. In their first Standby Phase, activate Opening from hand.
2. If it resolves, place Problem from Deck, summon Enlisted to the Extra Monster
   Zone, and place Bastion. Resolve the Bastion and Shatranga triggers in their
   own Chains to find a Trap.
3. Track the Synchro-only restriction through the end of your next turn. The
   searched Verdict normally waits to be Set and activated legally. If Opening
   was stopped, hold independent hand interaction and look for Problem access
   on your turn.

## Going first and going second

Going first, choose one engine route before spending a search or discarding
a Trap. Keep a zone for the next placement and use Set Dominus Traps to
preserve access to monster effects. Going second, assess Opening in the
Standby Phase, then decide whether to remove cards with a fielded Synchro or
set up more Spell/Trap pressure. Resolve hand traps that the chosen Dominus
would lock before committing to the permanent restriction.

## Opponent-turn interaction

Impulse answers effects that include Special Summons and Purge answers
Deck-to-hand effects. Spark requires a prior monster effect in hand or GY for
hand activation. Check whether you have a Trap in the GY for each bonus or
drawback. Verdict scales from a face-up negate to broad banish at five
Angelechy Monster Cards on the field. Endgame Problem can assign zones from
two placed monsters and can banish a summoned group from four. Check trigger
timing and available columns before Disturbance or Enlisted control changes.

## Resource and timing ledger

Track Monster Cards on the field separately from placed cards in your
Spell/Trap Zones. Track named effects per turn, actual control changes, zones,
and Extra Deck copies. Opposition restricts the entire turn, Score restricts
the rest of its resolution turn, and Opening's restriction lasts through
your next turn. Hand-activated Impulse, Spark, and Purge leave permanent
attribute restrictions. Log the combined set. Track when Score entered the
GY, face-down banishment from Desires, and whether a Synchro was properly
summoned before considering revival from the GY.

## Sideboarding and matchup notes

Board Purulia against hand-heavy summon sequences, Nibiru against early
summon counts, and Inspector Boarder when going first against decks that
need monster effects before establishing distinct Extra Deck types.
Space Trunade and Pot of Sloth help against established boards. A third
Desires trades selection for greater face-down banish exposure. Bifrost and
Brilliant Move can trade hand interaction for engine access. Recheck the
three-copy limit across all sections and preserve the 40/15/15 sizes.
