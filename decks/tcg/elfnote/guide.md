# Ars Magna Elfnote playbook

## Overview

40 Main / 15 Extra / 15 Side. [YDK](deck.ydk) · [Gameplay data](deck.json) ·
[Event list](README.md). Elfnote turns center-zone access into Synchro plays
and opponent-turn summons. Ars Magna and Medius provide a second route to
Link and Rank 8 interaction. The order of those routes matters when Elfnote
Power Patron or Strelitzia restricts the Extra Deck.

## Synergies

Lucina and Fortuna enter through the center. Once they are off center and have
another monster to swap with, they can provide opponent-turn removal. Regina
triggers when summoned to the center. Moving a Regina already on the field there
does not create a summon trigger. June Pride converts center access into up to
three bodies and can place Regina directly in that zone.

Choose non-Synchro lines before using Strelitzia's Quick Effect or putting the
restricting Tuner on field. Xyz Summon Lars from two Level 6 bodies or Photon Lord
from two Level 8 bodies only when the restriction permits. Citrinitas plus a
Power Patron Link makes qualifying Ars Magna removal effects available at Quick
Effect speed. Verify the original name and any required target before activation.

## Combo lines

**Lucina to center June Pride.** Start with Lucina in hand, a free center zone,
an unused Normal Summon, and a Deck copy of Elfnote Power Patron. June Pride
must be available and the two summons must be legal.

1. Special Summon Lucina to the center. Use its Main Phase effect to add Elfnote
   Power Patron.
2. Normal Summon the Level 1 Tuner in another Main Monster Zone. Use its Quick
   Effect targeting center Lucina. After Lucina becomes Level 9, Synchro Summon
   June Pride with Lucina and the Tuner. Place June Pride in the vacated center.
3. Use the Tuner's GY trigger to add Rhapsodia for Set follow-up, or an Elfnote
   extender for the hand. Hold June Pride if the board is stable.
4. In a later Main Phase, use June Pride's Quick Effect to return it to Extra.
   If still available, choose Regina from Deck for the center and Lucina from
   GY for a noncenter zone. After that Chain, Regina can summon a different
   Elfnote from Deck. Choose Fortuna or a Tuner only after checking current locks.
5. If Lucina's search is stopped, use another legal Tuner already in hand if
   available or keep Lucina and hand interaction. If the level-change target
   moves or the effect is negated, recalculate before attempting a summon.

**Ars Magna into Photon Lord.** Start with Finite in hand and a legal separate
Fusion or Link summon available. Corruption must remain in Deck, and a later
Link summon must also be available if needed to return Corruption.

1. Banish Finite to search Corruption. Banish Corruption to search Citrinitas.
2. After your qualifying Link summon, use both eligible banished monster triggers
   in a legal Chain order. A Fusion summon alone returns Finite and leaves
   Corruption banished.
3. If both Level 8 monsters resolve to the field and Xyz Summoning is permitted,
   overlay them into Photon Lord. Keep at least one material to negate a
   monster effect. If one body is removed, keep the other as a resource or
   use its appropriate removal effect with an established Power Patron Link.

## Going first and going second

Going first, preserve the center for the next trigger and keep at least one
layer of interaction after a stopped search. Choose June Pride if Lucina reaches
the Tuner and there is useful follow-up. Going second, use hand traps on effects
that lead to more bodies or interaction, then assess Kuibelt, Lars, S:P, or a
legally unlocked Ars Magna line. Use Terminus after deciding whether you need
attacks from non-Patron monsters this turn.

## Opponent-turn interaction

June Pride, Lucina, Fortuna and Strelitzia are live only in their stated windows
and positions. Effect Veiler is restricted to the opposing Main Phase. Rhapsodia
needs a legal GY target with a different original Attribute from the sent monster.
Photon Lord negates an opposing monster effect with material. S:P's temporary
banish needs two face-up monsters and at least one you control. Diabell can
convert a Spell/Trap into a small Synchro Tuner in response to an opposing
activation, but that summon does not itself negate the original effect.

## Resource and timing ledger

Track the center occupant, Normal Summon, per-name limits, GY and face-up
banishment, temporary Level changes, and which Extra Deck monsters were
properly summoned. Track whether a monster in GY is available to pay a cost or
is needed for a later revival. The Tuner's face-up Synchro restriction and
Strelitzia's lingering restriction are distinct. Record Terminus's attack limit
and any Dominus lock separately. Keep the required DARK Fairy and linked zones
for Purification and Mediclius.

## Sideboarding and matchup notes

Add Typhoon or Space Trunade against meaningful face-up backrow, Skull Meister
against GY effects, and Pot of Sloth when expecting several opposing permanents.
Dominus Impulse is strongest as a Set Trap in lines relying on LIGHT, EARTH or
WIND effects. Consider the cost of losing Fortuna, Regina, Veiler, Ogre, or
Finite effects before using it from hand. The third Impermanence is flexible
monster interaction. Keep 40/15/15 and check combined copy limits.

## Agent piloting checklist

1. Follow the [shared protocol](../../../docs/agent-play.md) and
   [TCG profile](../../../rules/tcg.md). Identify the center occupant and any
   summon locks before suggesting a play.
2. Check exact materials, final Levels, opponent response windows and trigger
   locations. Make the intended placement explicit.
3. Compare a stable endpoint plus hand interaction with a longer line exposed to
   another interruption. Keep opponent-private information out of the decision.
4. Verify attack restrictions, original ATK, and remaining battle damage
   before committing to a finish.

## Uncertainties and validation

The list matches the event publication by section and multiplicity. The
inventory and combo traces were reviewed against the included card texts.
They have no rules-engine simulation. Confirm simultaneous trigger order and
placement with the referee when multiple summons or control effects compete.
