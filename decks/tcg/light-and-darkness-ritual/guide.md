# Ars Magna Power Patron Light and Darkness Ritual playbook

## Overview

60 Main / 15 Extra / 15 Side. [YDK](deck.ydk) · [Gameplay data](deck.json) ·
[Event list](README.md). Use the Ritual package for removal, Spell recovery and
Mind Shuffle pressure. Medius, Ars Magna and Pendulum effects offer a second
route when the Ritual pieces are split across zones. This exact build includes
one Light and Darkness Ritual, one of each named Ritual Monster, and no Jupiter.

## Synergies

Preserve the lone Ritual Spell and legal tribute material. Magician protects
face-up Mind Shuffle from opposing destruction or banishment. Use a Ritual
Summon first when later GY revival matters. Mind Shuffle can ignore summon
conditions for a monster from hand. Record how that monster entered play.

Ars Magna monsters banished from hand can return on qualifying summons.
Citrinitas enables Quick Effects while a Power Patron Link is present.
For Vidolium's own summon, count the Fusion, Synchro and Xyz separately.
Choosing that summon can clear resources needed for Ritual follow-up. Nerva,
Junora and Photon Lord are routes to the three types when each is available.
Jupredo can search or remove cards here, while its named Xyz is absent.

## Combo lines

**Pendulum Treasure into Junora.** Start with Treasure, free Main Monster Zones,
Nervedo and Junoldo in Deck, and Junora available in Extra. Confirm that paying
the top three cards face-down will leave enough Deck and that the one-of risk
is acceptable.

1. Use Treasure to add Nervedo from Deck to the Extra Deck face-up.
2. On the next eligible Chain, use Nervedo's trigger to Special Summon Junoldo
   from Deck.
3. Use Junoldo's monster effect. Banish the top three cards face-down as cost.
   On resolution, destroy Junoldo and summon Junora as a Synchro Summon.
4. Check Junora's on-summon negate when the opponent has face-up cards. Use
   Junoldo's face-up Extra Deck trigger to add Nervedo from Extra to hand
   if it is still there. End on Junora with Nervedo available as a scale.
5. If Nervedo's summon effect is stopped, keep it face-up in Extra for another
   eligible route. If Junoldo's effect is stopped, preserve Junoldo as material
   when possible. Track the three cards already banished if the cost was paid.

**Ragged Records into a Level 8 Ritual.** Start with Records, Light and Darkness
Ritual and Griffoh in hand. At least one Ritual boss must remain in Deck.

1. Reveal the Ritual Spell for Records and add Magician of Dark Chaos or Black
   Luster Soldier. Pick Magician for Spell recovery and backrow protection, or
   Soldier for immediate targeted banish on summon.
2. Activate the Ritual Spell. If it resolves, use Griffoh in hand as the
   entire Tribute for that one Level 8 Ritual Summon.
3. Use the selected monster's summon trigger only with a valid choice. Magician
   can recover Records or another Spell in GY. Soldier can target an opposing
   card to banish. If the search was stopped, hold the Ritual Spell until you have
   a boss or use Griffoh to Set Mind Shuffle if available.

## Going first and going second

Going first, build a Patron line that can survive a stopped starter. Leave
Mind Shuffle room and a qualifying Level 7+ monster if you plan to swap.
Going second, compare Trunade, Pot of Sloth, Thrust, and the Bandit Tribute
Summon before investing in an Extra Deck line. Preserve the Normal Summon if
Bandit can remove a key opposing monster. Check Terminus before making a
battle plan with non-Patrons.

## Opponent-turn interaction

Mind Shuffle can add and discard or swap in separate Chains. Check the
opponent's activation, the return cost and the different-name condition for
the swap. Nervedo in scale can protect an eligible monster effect from an
opponent's chained monster effect. Philosophorum needs enough Ars Magna
monsters in legal zones. Diactorus needs three different Types, Photon Lord
needs material, and A Bao A Qu needs a discard. Magician and Soldier's
protection still permits opposing activated effects that target them.

## Resource and timing ledger

Record the lone Ritual Spell's location, both Ritual names and any proper
summon history. Track original Types, face-up Extra Deck Pendulums, face-down
banishments, summon counts, free Link destinations and used per-name effects.
Track the lasting attack restriction from Terminus, face-up banish triggers,
the restriction on the name Witch searches, and the activation turn of
anything Set by Thrust. Confirm when a Pendulum used as a cost will reach
face-up Extra versus the GY.

## Sideboarding and matchup notes

The side has three each of Ash Blossom, Fuwalos, Ghost Ogre, Nibiru, and
Typhoon. Favor them when the matchup exposes their specific windows. Keep the
Ritual targets and enough Patron extenders for the lines you intend to play.
Typhoon needs face-up backrow, and Nibiru can take your own monsters with the
opponent's. Maintain 60/15/15 and verify combined copy limits for any swaps.

1. Use the [shared protocol](../../../docs/agent-play.md) and
   [TCG profile](../../../rules/tcg.md). Check exact names and zones for each
   effect, especially Black Chaos versus Magician of Dark Chaos.
2. Confirm summon history and costs before proposing a Ritual, Patron or
   Extra Deck line. Account for alternate routes before spending the lone pieces.
3. Let every legal response window resolve. Use only permitted information and
   keep human move selection with the human.
4. Before battle, check attacks remaining, current ATK, immunity clauses and
   whether any removal or Tribute line improves the outcome.
