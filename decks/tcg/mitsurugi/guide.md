# Pure Mitsurugi playbook

## Overview

40 Main / 15 Extra / 15 Side. [YDK](deck.ydk) · [Gameplay data](deck.json) ·
[Deck source](README.md). Ritual Summon Murakumo and Futsu, then use a properly
summoned Ritual as Tribute to search and bring it back. Leave Great
Purification Set with a Level 8 Reptile available to pay for it. The October
19, 2026 banlist forbids Habakiri. The lines below use the cards in this list.

## Synergies

A properly summoned Level 8 Ritual can pay for a second Ritual Summon and
return on its Tribute trigger. Leave at least one Level 5+ Reptile if Great
Purification is the intended interaction. When recycling a spent Spell with
Kusanagi, consider which effect has already been used and whether the card is
for the current turn or follow-up.

A separate Normal Summon of Kusanagi is available after first using Aramasa
and Saji as Deck materials. The two Level 8s can also make a Rank 8. Overlaying
them spends both bodies, and detaching a material does not trigger Tribute
effects. Check which job the Rank 8 performs before converting the board.

## Combo lines

The traces assume no interruptions and an empty starting field on your side.
All listed Deck cards are available at the time of use. Other cards in hand
are held. Zone numbers designate example placements. CL means Chain Link.
Ritual material is Tributed during resolution unless the step specifically
calls it an activation cost or a Summoning procedure.

### Ragged Records of Rites plus Mitsurugi Ritual

Go first with these two cards and three other cards in hand. Start at 8000 LP.

1. Activate **Ragged Records of Rites**. Reveal **Mitsurugi Ritual** in hand
   and add **Murakumo** from Deck to hand. Ragged Records goes to GY.
2. Activate the first **Mitsurugi Ritual** with its hand-summon effect.
   Tribute **Aramasa** and **Saji** (Level 4 each) from Deck to GY and
   Ritual Summon **Murakumo** from hand to Monster Zone 2 in Attack Position.
   With an empty opposing field, pass on Murakumo's summon trigger.
3. Start a new Chain with **Saji** at CL1 and **Aramasa** at CL2. Resolve
   Aramasa to add **Kusanagi**, then Saji to add a second **Mitsurugi Ritual**.
   Both adds are from Deck to hand.
4. Use the second **Mitsurugi Ritual** with its Deck-summon effect. Tribute
   Level 8 **Murakumo** from field to GY. Ritual Summon **Futsu** from Deck
   to Monster Zone 3 in Defense Position.
5. In the new Chain, activate **Murakumo** at CL1 and **Ragged Records** at
   CL2. Murakumo left the field by the Ritual Spell's effect, and only
   Futsu remains face-up on your side. Return Ragged Records from GY to hand,
   then add **Great Purification** from Deck and revive Murakumo from GY in
   Monster Zone 2 in Attack Position.
6. Normal Summon **Kusanagi** to Monster Zone 4 in Attack Position. Target
   the first **Mitsurugi Ritual** in GY and recover it to hand. Set **Great
   Purification** in Spell & Trap Zone 3 and pass.

| --- | --- |
| Monster Zones | Murakumo (2, ATK), Futsu (3, DEF), Kusanagi (4, ATK) |
| Spell & Trap Zones | Set Great Purification (3) |
| Hand | The three held cards, Ragged Records, and the recovered first Ritual |
| Graveyard | Saji, Aramasa, second Ritual |
| Deck / Extra Deck / banishment | 28 Main, 15 Extra, nothing banished |

**Watch for** removal before the second Ritual can use the Level 8 on field.
You can also start with Ritual and Murakumo in hand, skip the Ragged Records
steps, and end with one fewer card in hand and 29 cards in Deck.

### Wousu plus Saji plus Mitsurugi Ritual

Go second with these three cards and three other cards in hand after the
normal draw. Start at 8000 LP. Assume the opponent controls a legal tribute
and at least one other monster that Murakumo can destroy.

1. Special Summon **Wousu** from hand by Tributing **Saji** from hand and
   one opposing monster as the Summoning procedure. Put Wousu in Monster
   Zone 2 in Attack Position. You can only Special Summon Reptiles and
   activate Reptile monster effects for the rest of the turn.
2. Use the **Saji** Tribute trigger in a new Chain. Add **Prayers** from
   Deck to hand.
3. Activate the first **Mitsurugi Ritual** with its Deck-summon effect.
   Tribute Level 8 **Wousu** from field to GY and Ritual Summon **Murakumo**
   from Deck to Monster Zone 2 in Attack Position. Leave Wousu's optional
   recovery unused. Activate **Murakumo** in the following Chain to destroy
   the remaining opposing monsters.
4. Activate **Prayers**, Tributing **Murakumo** from field to GY as cost.
   Choose just the search and add **Futsu**. After Prayers resolves, use
   Murakumo's Tribute trigger to add a second **Mitsurugi Ritual** from
   Deck and revive Murakumo in Monster Zone 2 in Attack Position.
5. Activate the second **Mitsurugi Ritual**, choosing its hand-summon
   effect. Tribute Level 4 **Aramasa** and **Kusanagi** from Deck to GY and
   Ritual Summon **Futsu** from hand to Monster Zone 3 in Defense Position.
6. In the next Chain, put **Kusanagi** at CL1 targeting **Wousu** in GY
   and **Aramasa** at CL2. Resolve the search for a second **Saji** and
   return Wousu to hand. Hold both for follow-up and pass in this example.

### Pre-Preparation of Rites plus Mitsurugi Prayers

Go first with these two cards and three other cards in hand. Start at 8000 LP.

1. Activate **Pre-Preparation of Rites**, adding **Mirror** and **Murakumo**
   from Deck to hand.

   Choose both effects. Add **Saji**, take 800 damage, then Special Summon

   **Futsu** with Murakumo and **Mitsurugi Ritual** with Saji, both from
   Deck. Murakumo remains in GY because it has not been Ritual Summoned yet.

## Going first and going second

Going first, use Ritual materials from the Deck when your hand provides a
Level 8. Prioritize Great Purification with a live Tribute and keep both
Rituals for repeatable interaction when you can. A set Impulse preserves
the effects of your LIGHT, EARTH, and WIND monsters.

Going second, use Fuwalos before putting a card on your field if the matchup
calls for it. Decide whether Wousu or Murakumo clears the monsters you need
to remove before spending a Level 8 on a Rank 8. Droplet can protect a
sequence against existing monster effects, and Magatama can remove a face-up
Spell/Trap. Check the damage restriction before making Exciton or using
S:P's first effect.

## Opponent-turn interaction

- **Great Purification** Tributes a Level 5+ Reptile as cost. If you Tribute a
  properly summoned Murakumo or Futsu and its Tribute effect is available,
  use the trigger on the next Chain to add a card and then revive it.
- **Murakumo** can make the opponent discard a card or have their effect
  negated. Save it for an activation worth spending the once-per-turn effect.
- **Futsu** can revive an eligible Reptile after an opposing Special Summon.
  Revive Saji for a Spell/Trap, Aramasa for monster access, or Kusanagi for
  recursion. An eligible Murakumo in the GY gives you a board wipe trigger.
- **Dyna Mondo** can revive a previously Ritual Summoned Murakumo during the
  opponent's turn. Check targets and the resulting new Chain.
- **I:P** can Link Summon Fancy Ball during the opponent's Main Phase if you
  have sufficient material. Fancy Ball then has its current-turn monster
  effect negate available. Both this play and S:P use DARK monsters.

## Resource and timing ledger

Track both effects of Ritual individually and whether each search name has
already used its summon-or-Tribute effect. Record proper Ritual Summon status
and exact copies in each zone. Mirror can Ritual Summon from the GY.
Prayers, Futsu, and Great Purification can only revive Ritual Monsters whose
summon history allows it. A monster used as material for a Link or Xyz
Summon does not count as Tributed.

Record 2000 LP as Ruler's activation cost, and 800 damage during the
resolution of the summon option of Prayers. Record Wousu's turn-long
Reptile restriction and Impulse's rest-of-Duel attribute restriction.
Under both restrictions Gorgon is a viable DARK Reptile Link if the legal
materials and zones are available. Haggard is a WATER Reptile.

## Agent piloting checklist

1. Use the [shared protocol](../../../docs/agent-play.md) and
   [TCG Advanced rules](../../../rules/tcg.md). Read card text before
   declaring and preserve every legal response window.
2. Identify whether you can supply a Level 8 in hand or field for the
   Deck mode, or can make the hand mode live with Deck materials.
3. State which effect is chosen, which cards move and from where to where.
   Track activation costs separately from the resolving effect and order
   subsequent simultaneous triggers.
4. Verify valid targets, free zones, starting LP, and Ritual Summon
   history. Recalculate if anything in the example changed.
5. Answer the human's choices from their permitted view and apply only
   actions that they have declared or delegated.

## Uncertainties and validation

The list is from a published community deck. The combo traces are reviewed
against card text and the official Ritual mechanics examples, with no duel
simulation. Konami's
[Mitsurugi product guide](https://img.yugioh-card.com/en/downloads/product_guides/L26D/en/LD26_Mitsurugi_guide.pdf)
demonstrates Tribute triggers from Deck and an initial Ritual Summon through
Mirror from the GY. The
[Ruler ruling reference](https://ftp.yugipedia.com/wiki/Card_Rulings:Ruler_of_the_End_of_the_World)
documents declaring a Ritual option at activation. Confirm with the referee
when an unusual replacement effect, trigger conflict, or restriction changes
a listed step.
