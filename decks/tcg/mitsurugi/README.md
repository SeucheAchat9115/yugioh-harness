# Pure Mitsurugi

Version 1 imports the 40 Main / 15 Extra / 15 Side community list by
Unchained Soul King, retrieved on November 15, 2026. Mitsurugi is the
Ritual engine. Ruler of the End of the World supports it by applying a
Ritual Spell's effect from the Deck.
[YGOPRODeck list](https://ygoprodeck.com/deck/pure-mitsurugi-post-october-2026-banlist-739960)

The YDK preserves the source order, quantities, and alternate-art passcodes.
Copies were checked under the [October 19, 2026 TCG Advanced banlist](../../../rules/banlists/tcg-2026-10-19.json).
Konami confirms TCG printings of Ruler of the End of the World (passcode
3739500) and W:P Fancy Ball in
[Magnificent Monsters](https://www.db.yugioh-card.com/yugiohdb/card_search.action?ope=1&pid=2000001680000&request_locale=en&rp=99999).

Gameplay records reuse YGOPRODeck API data from the repository where available,
with remaining records from
[Project Ignis BabelCDB](https://github.com/ProjectIgnis/BabelCDB/tree/6e21009855ed5f3b5966a24545bd0db393d44697).
The English TCG name and wording of Ruler of the End of the World follow
Konami's TCG database.
