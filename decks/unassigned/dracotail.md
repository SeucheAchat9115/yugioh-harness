# Dracotail agent playbook

## Overview

Exact list: **40 Main / 15 Extra / 15 Side**, with 36 distinct card records.
[Gameplay JSON](dracotail.json) · [Original YDK](dracotail.ydk).
Format and banlist remain unassigned; verify legality before playing.

The engine turns hand/field Fusion Materials into searchable set Dracotail cards
and removal, then repeats Fusion Summons in the opposing Main Phase. Lukias/Ketu
provide access, Rahu supplies a Deck material, and Faimena/Mululu provide Quick
Fusions. The many hand traps are also potential hand materials, so each expenditure
changes both defense and future Fusion access. Fenrir offers an independent threat;
King of the Swamp and the single Polymerization open named-substitute Fusions.

## Card roles and usage

**Starters/access:** Lukias, Ketu, Rahu, Fenrir, and conditional King-to-Polymerization.
**Fusion enablers:** Faimena, fielded Mululu, Rahu, Ketu when the opponent controls
a monster, and Polymerization. **Materials with payoffs:** each named Dracotail
monster sets a Spell/Trap only when sent to GY as Fusion Material. Mululu adds
negation, Urgula adds backrow destruction, and Pan adds monster destruction.
**Interaction/follow-up:** Gulamel, Arthalion, Flame, Horn, and the hand traps.
Keep enough separate materials after paying Faimena's discard.

## Synergies

- **Rahu + Dracotail material riders:** use a Deck Dracotail with a hand monster
  to convert the Fusion into set cards. The Extra Deck requirement still mandates
  a monster **in hand** for Gulamel and Arthalion; Rahu is not an empty-hand starter.
- **Lukias + Faimena:** a Normal Summon can find the Quick Fusion, but Faimena
  discards itself as cost. You still need Lukias plus a separate hand material.
- **Gulamel + a Dracotail activation:** direct Quick Effect response can destroy
  one targeted opposing card. A set card is not an activation until it is actually
  activated. Use the allowed chain window; do not insert the effect mid-resolution.
- **Mululu/Urgula/Pan + Fusion:** resolve material triggers afterward. Their
  negate/destruction riders have no targeting language; select at resolution,
  and only apply after the preceding Set succeeds. Do not treat them as omni-negates.
- **Arthalion + several hand materials:** more hand materials increase its target
  cap, but consume hand traps and follow-up. A Deck/field material does not increase it.
- **King + a real Dragon Effect Monster:** can substitute Dark Magician for Dragoon.
  The substitute remains an Effect Monster, so this line used zero Normal Monsters;
  Dragoon has zero uses of its Normal-material-dependent destruction effect.
- **Polymerization + Chimera:** supports its Spell-Fusion draw/destruction and GY
  targeting protection. Faimena/Ketu cannot summon the Beast Chimera because their
  Fusion choices are restricted to Dragon/Spellcaster; Mululu/Rahu require Dracotail.

## Combo lines

### D1 — Rahu + Urgula in hand → Gulamel, Horn, and Flame

**Inputs:** Rahu and Urgula in hand, Mululu still in Deck, Gulamel in Extra, and
Horn/Flame in Deck. No banish/replacement effect prevents materials reaching GY.

1. Activate Rahu and allow responses. On resolution use Mululu from Deck as the
   Dracotail material and Urgula from hand as the mandatory hand material.
2. Fusion Summon Gulamel. In the resulting trigger window activate Mululu and
   Urgula's distinct material effects; set Horn and Flame in a chosen order.
3. If a valid opposing face-up monster/backrow exists when the appropriate rider
   resolves, optionally negate/destroy it. Do not promise removal on an empty field.
4. Pass with Gulamel and the traps, retaining the unused Normal Summon for any
   additional legal development. Opponent-turn Dracotail activation can enable
   Gulamel's destruction; newly set traps do not activate on the setting turn.

**Output:** Gulamel + Horn/Flame, subject to responses and remaining Deck copies.
Rahu locks subsequent Extra Deck summons to Fusions. Swapping Urgula for a generic
hand monster gives only Mululu's one set; swapping it for Pan gives a monster-destroy
rider instead. This line requires Rahu **and** a hand monster, not Rahu alone.
**Failure/fallback:** Ash can stop Rahu. Without enough hand/field material, preserve
hand traps/Faimena for a later line instead of pretending Deck-only material works.

### D2 — Lukias + Pan in hand → opposing-turn Arthalion and material interaction

1. Normal Summon Lukias; search Faimena on its summon trigger. End your turn with
   Lukias on field, Pan and Faimena still in hand, and relevant resources unused.
2. During opponent Main Phase, discard Faimena as cost and declare its Quick Fusion.
   On resolution use Lukias from field + Pan from hand to Fusion Summon Arthalion.
3. Arthalion can target up to one monster on field/GY because one material came
   from hand. Lukias and Pan's material effects can set Horn/Flame; Pan can also
   destroy a monster after its Set resolves. Order your simultaneous triggers legally.

**Output:** Arthalion, conditional bounce/destruction, and newly set traps for a
later turn. Those traps cannot immediately activate on the opponent turn they
were set. Faimena discarded for cost does **not** add a third set effect.
**Failure/fallback:** if Lukias is removed before the Quick Fusion, the specified
materials are gone. Rebuild a legal line from actual hand/field resources or pass;
this is an exposed waiting line, not a guaranteed first-turn board.

### D3 — Polymerization + King + Mululu/Pan/Urgula → Dragoon or Tyrant Dragon

**Inputs:** Polymerization already in hand, King still in hand/field, and a real
Dragon Effect Monster in hand/field. No active Type/summon restriction prevents it.

1. For Dragoon, substitute King for the specifically named Dark Magician; use the
   actual Dragon Effect Monster as the other material. Fusion Summon via Polymerization.
2. For Blue-Eyes Tyrant Dragon, substitute King for Blue-Eyes White Dragon and use
   a real Dragon as the other material. Choose based on a negate versus combat/trap recovery.
3. The Dracotail Dragon sent as material can set a Dracotail card and apply its
   appropriate rider. Keep another hand card if planning to pay Dragoon's negate.

**Output:** selected Fusion plus material follow-up. Dragoon has no Normal-material
monster-destruction activations from this line. King cannot replace a generic
Type requirement. If you discarded the only King to search Polymerization,
you need **another** substitute for this line; Polymerization cannot use that GY King.

### D4 — Polymerization with three different names → Guardian Chimera

**Inputs:** Polymerization and three different named monsters across hand and
field, at least one from each. Enough opposing cards must be available for its
applicable destruction effect; check the precise activation/resolution rules.

1. Use two hand + one field, or one hand + two field materials, for Chimera.
2. Its Spell-Fusion trigger draws by hand-material count and destroys by field-material
   count. Dracotail monsters used as materials provide their own separate triggers.
3. Polymerization now in GY gives Chimera targeting protection while it remains there.

**Output:** Chimera plus conditional draws, destruction, and Dracotail sets.
King can fetch Polymerization, but the discarded King is not a material for it.
Do not substitute Faimena/Ketu/Rahu/Mululu as the summoning effect for this line.

### D5 — established Arthalion + Pan + Faimena → Filia Regis

**Inputs:** Arthalion (Dragon Fusion) on field, Pan (Level 7 Dragon) and Faimena
in hand, Filia in Extra. This assumes the initial Arthalion was established separately.

1. In a Main Phase, discard Faimena; Fusion Summon Filia using Arthalion from field
   plus Pan from hand. Both of Filia's requirements are now satisfied.
2. Pan's material effect can set a Dracotail card and destroy a monster if legal.
3. Hold Filia's Main Phase Quick banish. If it banishes a field card, return one of
   your Dragons afterward; account for losing/recycling your own body.

**Output:** Filia with conditional Pan follow-up. Do not promise Arthalion's revival
from the same send event without a confirmed ruling. The safe revival discussion
below only assumes an eligible copy was already in GY before a later multi-send event.

## Going first and going second

**First:** decide whether Fenrir's empty-monster-field Special Summon is useful
before Normal Summoning Lukias. Search Faimena only if separate materials remain;
otherwise search the missing engine piece. Rahu supplies one Deck piece but requires
hand material. End on live interactions and follow-up rather than putting every
hand trap into a large Arthalion. Newly set Horn/Flame are usable on the next turn,
but traps set during that next turn's Fusion will wait again.

**Second:** use eligible Lightning Storm before any face-up cards; Fenrir before
other monsters if desired. Ketu's optional Fusion becomes available when the opponent
controls a monster. Choose Mululu/Pan/Urgula according to negate/monster/backrow
needs. Arthalion can bounce GY resources as well as board monsters. Against towers,
check targeting, destruction, and activation restrictions before choosing removal.
Verify battle positions, actual ATK, damage prevention, and responses for lethal.

## Opponent-turn interaction

Faimena and fielded Mululu operate in Main Phase, not in Battle Phase by default.
Gulamel reacts to your Dracotail card/effect activation; choose a legal direct response
window. Horn is targeting bounce of an Attack Position monster, not all-position
removal. Flame negates a face-up Spell's effects until end of turn, not its activation.
Fenrir's attack/opposing-monster-effect trigger starts at the appropriate trigger
window; it does not negate the triggering effect.

Against Branded, Ash on Branded Fusion is a high-impact candidate. Crow can remove
Branded in Red's GY target or an Albaz material before a later Fusion resolves.
Belle may answer a GY-banishing/recovery effect where its text and timing permit.
Crow on an Albion sent for Mirrorjade cost denies the later GY payoff if it stays
banished, but **does not stop Mirrorjade's banish**. Ogre destroys a field card but
does not generally negate its effect. Check Lost's Fusion response protection
before announcing a response in the post-Fusion window.

Fuwalos requires control of **no cards** at activation, not just no monsters; use
it before any set/face-up card prevents activation. Draw once per qualifying summon
event, and apply its random End Phase correction instead of keeping all excess cards.

## Resource and timing ledger

Track each named Dracotail's individual effects across all copies. Each material
Set effect is once per turn per name: two Mululu copies do not yield two Mululu sets.
Faimena's Fusion and its material Set are distinct effects, but cost discards are
not material sends. Track Ketu/Rahu/Horn/Flame activation limits and remaining single
copies of Polymerization/Horn/Flame in Deck before selecting a search/set.

Rahu and Mululu impose Fusion-only Extra Deck restrictions **after resolution**;
Fusion restriction persists for the turn even if the originating monster leaves.
S:P, Seal, Spirit, and TY-PHON are unavailable under that active lock. TY-PHON's
alternate procedure stops subsequent Normal/Special Summons; use it only with the
required two opposing Extra Deck summons and after checking all other restrictions.
Spirit's Dragon-only restriction prevents Spellcaster Fusion choices while applying.

Record Arthalion's hand-material count, targets, properly Fusion Summoned history,
and whether a revived Arthalion/Gulamel must banish on leaving. Only rely on GY
revival from a multi-monster send when the triggering copy was already in GY unless
same-event arrival has been adjudicated. A monster improperly sent from Extra to GY
cannot simply be revived. Link materials and Faimena discards do not trigger the
Fusion-Material Dracotail riders. Mysterion cannot be used as Fusion Material.

Track Magnamhut's delayed End Phase search, Fuwalos's draw/hand-correction state,
Dogwood's LP gained versus halve-LP condition, and Droll's symmetric add-to-hand
restriction. When recycling, keep a distinct-name Fusion plan and an unused Dracotail
set target available rather than blindly choosing the strongest printed ATK.

## Sideboarding and matchup notes

For going second, Lightning Storm/Duster/Pankratops are conditional board breakers;
activate Storm before face-up development. Droll is valuable against repeated Main
Deck adds, but also stops your Lukias/Ketu/King/Fenrir adds during its duration.
Lancea interferes with both players' banishing. Dogwood buys LP against effect-monster
summon sequences and can halve your LP if the opponent declines to trigger gains.
The extra Pan improves monster-destruction material access when that is relevant.

Consider replacing lower-impact hand traps for the observed matchup before removing
core Fusion access. Preserve **40/15/15** sizes and legal total copy limits after
swaps. The side Pan makes two accessible only after a swap; do not assume it exists
in Main during Game 1. Against Branded, protect GY interaction and answers to Fusion
access. If expecting backrow, retain Urgula and breakers. No fixed swap count or
win rate is asserted without a selected format and observed opponent list.

## Agent piloting checklist

Follow the [shared play protocol](../../docs/agent-play.md):

1. Use own hand and public information; separate an inferred opposing response
   from a revealed card. Read the full JSON text for the selected card.
2. Check empty-field conditions before Fenrir/Fuwalos/Storm, then reserve the
   Normal Summon and choose which material effects the state needs.
3. For each Fusion, explicitly list hand/field/Deck sources, the mandatory hand
   material, Faimena's separate discard, material names/Types, and active locks.
4. Declare activation costs/targets, allow responses, then choose resolution-time
   materials legally. Recompute if a monster leaves or a GY replacement applies.
5. After each chain, collect material/Fusion triggers in the permitted window,
   track which sets succeeded, and do not activate traps set that turn.
6. Before passing, enumerate live hand/field Quick Effects, usable set cards,
   GY recursion, and delayed End Phase obligations; retain materials to execute them.

## Uncertainties and validation

Reviewed against the stored names, Types, material requirements, effect text, and
counts. The audit verifies exact inventory/hash/document structure, not simulated
duel legality. No combo has been executed in a rules engine.

Format/banlist legality, same-event GY arrival for Arthalion/Gulamel revival,
material-substitution edge cases, and protected post-Fusion response windows need
agreed rulings before dependent actions. Safe substitute examples here use King
from hand/field with Polymerization, not from Deck. Spirit with Eyes of Blue has
no Mausoleum target in this exact list; that effect is unavailable. These limitations
are part of the guide rather than silently fixed by adding absent cards.
