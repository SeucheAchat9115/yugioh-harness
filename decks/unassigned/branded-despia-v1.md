# Branded Despia agent playbook

## Overview

Exact list: **53 Main / 14 Extra / 12 Side**, with 50 distinct card records.
[Gameplay JSON](branded-despia-v1.json) · [Original YDK](branded-despia-v1.ydk).
Format and banlist are unassigned; this is strategic preparation, not a legality certification.

The plan is to establish an Albaz Fusion, turn Extra Deck sends into End Phase
resources, and keep a second Fusion/removal action for the opposing turn. Mirrorjade
is the usual interaction anchor. Cartesia/Quem and the Branded/Bystial GY engines
provide alternate development and follow-up. This exact 53-card list has fewer
Branded Fusion copies than its many access cards; avoid spending both without recovery.
There are no Polymerization, Branded Beast, Gimmick Puppet Nightmare, or Flip monsters
in the list. Do not import combo assumptions from a different Branded build.

## Card roles and usage

**Starters:** Aluber/Opening access Branded Fusion; Fusion Deployment accesses Cartesia
or Albaz; High Spirits, Gold Sarcophagus and Foolish Burial provide conditional
indirect access. **Extenders:** Cartesia, Quem, Kitt, Ecclesia and the Bystials depend
on board/GY conditions. **Payoffs:** Mirrorjade, Rindbrumm and Fusion traps create
interaction; Albion/Titaniklad/Luluwalilith create later resources. **Utility:**
Super Polymerization and the Tactics cards are reactive to actual opponent actions.
Use the full card text before spending any card summarized below.

## Synergies

- **Opening + Tragedy:** Opening discards during its effect, so a discarded Tragedy
  can search after resolution. The cost discard of Lubellion/Albaz/Super Polymerization
  does not do this. Tragedy used as Branded Fusion material is also sent by effect.
- **Mirrorjade + an Extra Deck send:** Albion gives Branded access in End Phase;
  Titaniklad gives Albaz/Quem. Rindbrumm gives opponent-turn GY play. These are
  different delayed resources, not immediate searches when paid as Mirrorjade cost.
- **Cartesia + Granguignol + Luluwalilith:** Fusion development can send the Synchro
  without summoning it, then turn its GY End Phase effect into a LIGHT Spellcaster.
  Quem sees cards leaving either Extra Deck, creating additional revival windows.
- **Gold Sarcophagus + Mercourier/Tragedy:** banish triggers give immediate access;
  Gold Sarcophagus's ordinary recovery still waits two of your Standby Phases.
- **Shrouded/Saronir + Retribution:** load a wanted Branded Spell and Retribution
  separately, then use Retribution's GY effect to recover it. Do not claim a search
  when only the GY send has happened.
- **Regained + LIGHT/DARK banishment:** recycle and draw around Bystial/Fusion plays;
  avoid recycling the exact resource needed for another unresolved action.

## Combo lines

### B1 — Branded Fusion + one discard → Mirrorjade and a Despia search

**Inputs:** Branded Fusion in hand (or obtained via Aluber), one other discardable
card, Albaz and Tragedy accessible as Deck materials, Lubellion and Mirrorjade in
Extra Deck. No prior non-Fusion Extra Deck summon this turn. Assume effects resolve.

1. Activate Branded Fusion; allow responses. On resolution send Albaz + Tragedy
   from Deck and Fusion Summon Lubellion the Searing Dragon.
2. In the resulting trigger window, declare Tragedy and Lubellion effects in an
   allowed order. Pay Lubellion's discard at activation. Tragedy adds Aluber or Quem.
   The searched card is not available to pay a cost already due in this window.
3. Resolve Lubellion using itself on field plus Albaz in GY, shuffling both back
   as materials to Fusion Summon Mirrorjade.
4. Hold Mirrorjade until a meaningful banish window. Its cost can send Albion;
   register the End Phase Branded add/set when applicable.

**Output:** Mirrorjade, a searched Despia, and later access if Mirrorjade sends Albion.
The extra discard is spent; this is not an unconditional one-card line.
**Failure/fallback:** Ash on Branded Fusion stops the ladder. Lost does not stop
Ash's effect negation. If Lubellion is stopped, keep its body and use an available
Cartesia/Red line only after checking remaining materials and restrictions.

### B2 — Branded Fusion with a LIGHT → Albion route to Mirrorjade

**Inputs:** Branded Fusion, accessible Albaz + a LIGHT material (e.g. Quem), Albion
and Mirrorjade in Extra. No non-Fusion Extra Deck summons. No extra discard required.

1. Branded Fusion sends Albaz + Quem from Deck to summon Albion the Branded Dragon.
2. Activate Albion's Fusion trigger. Banish Albion from field and Albaz from GY
   as Mirrorjade's Fusion + Albaz materials.
3. Mirrorjade can send the **second** Albion copy as its cost for later End Phase access.

**Output:** Mirrorjade with the first Albion and Albaz banished; Quem is in GY.
The first Albion is not in GY, so it does not grant an End Phase search by itself.
**Failure/fallback:** banish restrictions or removal of Albaz break step 2; reassess
legal hand/field/GY materials instead of assuming the same endpoint.

### B3 — High Spirits + Albaz + LIGHT/DARK partner → Granguignol and End Phase value

1. Reveal Albaz for High Spirits; send Albion the Branded Dragon (Level 8 Dragon,
   2500 ATK) from Extra to GY. Apply the optional discard of Albaz and add Cartesia.
2. Special Summon Cartesia because Albaz is in GY.
3. In Main Phase, use Cartesia + the stated LIGHT/DARK partner to Fusion Summon
   Granguignol. If the partner is in hand, it is consumed; this line requires it.
4. Granguignol sends Luluwalilith or Titaniklad according to the desired follow-up.
   In End Phase, resolve the applicable GY effects: Albion's Branded access,
   the selected sent monster's payoff, and Cartesia's return if still in GY.

**Output:** Granguignol plus conditional End Phase resources; not automatically
Mirrorjade. Luluwalilith can summon Quem/Cartesia/Ecclesia, subject to remaining copies.
High Spirits can use only one of its effects that turn, so using its setup effect
precludes its same-turn GY return. Never send Luluwalilith with High Spirits itself:
it is a Synchro, while High Spirits requires a Fusion.

### B4 — Branded in Red → Guardian Chimera on the opposing turn

**Inputs:** a legally set Red available to activate; Despia/Albaz in your GY;
after recovery, three different monster names available with at least one from
hand and one from field. Chimera remains in Extra. Enough opposing cards exist for
its destruction effect to be legally applied; verify the exact resolution rules.

1. Activate Red targeting the stated GY monster. If the target leaves before
   recovery, do not assume the optional Fusion still occurs.
2. Recover it, then banish three correctly named hand/field materials for Chimera.
   Two hand + one field means draw two / destroy one; one hand + two field means
   draw one / destroy two when its Spell-Fusion trigger legally resolves.
3. Handle material triggers in the next window (e.g. Tragedy banished by effect).
   The Chimera cannot attack directly that turn due to Red.

**Output:** Chimera plus conditional draw/destruction. This list has no Polymerization
in GY to grant Chimera targeting protection. Super Polymerization cannot make
Chimera: it cannot supply a hand material. Cartesia's monster effect can summon
Chimera with correct materials but does not trigger its Spell-Fusion draw/destruction.

## Going first and going second

**First:** obtain Branded Fusion or a Cartesia route without overusing the Normal
Summon. Prefer interaction plus follow-up over an unprotected maximal board.
Mirrorjade with a live future cost and retained materials is more useful than a
spent Mirrorjade beside unusable set Fusions. Set Quick-Play spells/traps before
passing and identify which can actually activate next turn.

**Second:** inspect field Type/Attribute/name combinations for Super Polymerization
before selecting its target. Ecclesia/Albaz may convert opposing bodies into your
Fusions. Activate a relevant side breaker before committing resources; Dark Ruler
No More makes same-turn damage to the opponent zero. Thrust/Talent require actual
opposing monster effects in the specified windows. Check battle restrictions,
Mirrorjade uniqueness, and opposing responses before claiming lethal.

## Opponent-turn interaction

Use Mirrorjade's non-targeting banish on a meaningful body, not automatically the
first summon. Cost is paid before responses; banishing the sent Albion afterward
does not undo Mirrorjade, but can deny its later GY payoff. Mercourier needs a
controlled Albaz-material Fusion. Cartesia works only in Main Phase; Red/Banishment
require legal GY targets and actual remaining materials. Rindbrumm's field effect
covers Extra Deck monster types named in its text; it does not negate every monster.

Against Dracotail, Ash can stop Rahu's Deck-material effect or Lukias/Ketu access.
Rahu's material-location requirements remain even if access resolves. Material
riders that set cards and then negate/destroy are not searches to hand. Branded Lost
protects particular Fusion response windows, not every later chain of the turn.
Super Polymerization prevents responses to its activation, not all resulting triggers.

## Resource and timing ledger

Track name-wide usage for each relevant effect; **Aluber, Tragedy, High Spirits,
and Retribution each choose one effect per turn**, not both. Track both independent
effects of Cartesia, Quem, Mercourier, and Saronir separately. Branded Fusion and
Fusion Deployment restrict the entire turn: an earlier non-Fusion Extra Deck summon
makes their activation illegal. Opening/Lubellion restrict later Extra Deck summons.

For each Mirrorjade instance, track use and the next-turn restriction, who controls
it, whether properly Fusion Summoned, and eligibility for its opponent-caused
leave-field delayed wipe. Check any reset questions with the referee. Reserve a
legal Extra Deck send; Sanctifire/Alba-Lenatus cannot be used as Fusion Material.
A Fusion sent straight from Extra to GY is not properly summoned and cannot simply
be revived. Rindbrumm sent as setup can revive Albaz, but not revive itself without
its proper-summon history. Record Albion/Titaniklad/Luluwalilith/Cartesia/High Spirits
End Phase conditions and Gold Sarcophagus's second-Standby schedule.

## Sideboarding and matchup notes

These are conditional packages, not fixed swaps; agree on the format first.
Against Fusion engines, Barrier is strong but symmetric: plan to use already
established interaction rather than your own Fusion effects under it. Lancea can
stop opposing banish plans but also your Bystials and banishing Fusion routes.
Different Dimension Ground affects your material GY triggers too. Duster is for
backrow; Dark Ruler is for disabling monster boards before rebuilding.

Alba-Lenatus, Quaeritis, and Master of Ham are **Side Deck Fusion cards**, not
available in Game 1. Swap Extra Deck cards with Extra Deck side choices rather
than adding them to Main. Preserve the recorded 53/14/12 sizes after siding.
Alba-Lenatus is a conditional answer to Dragons; Master of Ham has no included
Flip target and needs opposing Beasts for an efficient Super Polymerization use.
Do not remove all Albaz access, the two Albion resource slots, or the material
needed for the intended Fusion route merely to add more reactive cards.

## Agent piloting checklist

Follow the [shared play protocol](../../docs/agent-play.md). Before each decision:

1. Read own hand and public state; never inspect private opponent cards/deck order.
2. Check Normal Summon, name-wide counters, current locks, legal Fusion materials,
   remaining Extra Deck costs, properly summoned GY targets, and response windows.
3. Compare a safe interaction/follow-up line with a damage line; choose based on
   public threats, LP, and available responses. A guide line is a candidate, not a script.
4. Declare costs/targets at activation, leave resolution-time materials undecided
   until required, allow responses, then update state before choosing the next action.
5. On interruption, recompute from surviving resources. Before passing, enumerate
   live opponent-turn actions and scheduled End Phase effects.

## Uncertainties and validation

Reviewed by tracing the stored card text and material identities. Inventory,
counts, and deck hash are mechanically audited. No duel engine or simulated
opponent validated these lines; success assumes the stated effects resolve.

Format/card legality, historical texts, precise Branded Lost response windows,
Fusion Duplication copying/cost/restriction interactions, and unusual summon/reset
cases require the agreed rules/referee. This list's unusual Side Extra choices
have not been replaced with standard cards. No absent lock monster is assumed.
