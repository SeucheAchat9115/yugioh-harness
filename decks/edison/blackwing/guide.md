# Blackwing — Edison playbook

## Overview

40 Main / 15 Extra / 15 Side. Exact list: [YDK](deck.ydk) and [gameplay data](deck.json).
Imported on 2026-10-05. This guide covers every included card; tactical lines are
conditional and preserve all opponent response opportunities. JSON stores current
YGOPRODeck text; Edison historical text/rulings take precedence as agreed in
[rules](../../../rules/edison.md). See the historical exceptions below before play.

Control tempo with Black Whirlwind, efficient Blackwing bodies, and traps; turn
battle wins or GY resources into Synchro pressure. This exact list has three
Blizzard, Shura, Kalut, Bora, Sirocco, and Icarus; only one Gale and one Vayu.
There is no Royal Oppression, Sangan, or additional Vayu engine in this list.

## Search, summon, and recovery map

- **Whirlwind → Deck to hand:** only your Blackwing Normal Summons trigger it;
  target selection is a search choice, not a targeted monster effect. Each copy
  produces its own trigger. The continuous Spell must remain applicable and the
  summoned monster must supply its ATK at resolution; review removal/Book responses.
  Use current ATK and remaining copies, with these printed-ATK baseline choices:
  Sirocco (2000) → Shura/Bora/Kalut/Blizzard/Gale/Vayu;
  Shura (1800) → Bora/Kalut/Blizzard/Gale/Vayu;
  Bora (1700) → Kalut/Blizzard/Gale/Vayu;
  Kalut (1400) → Blizzard/Gale/Vayu;
  Blizzard or Gale (1300) → Vayu; Vayu (800) → none.
- **Shura → Deck to field:** after battle destruction and sending that monster to
  GY, included summonable 1500-or-less targets are Kalut, Gale, and Vayu. Blizzard
  cannot be Special Summoned despite meeting the ATK threshold. Recruited effects
  are negated; being a Tuner is not negated. A recruited Vayu's field material
  restriction is negated while Shura's negation applies.
- **Blizzard → GY to field:** target Shura, Bora, Kalut, Gale, or Vayu (Level 4 or
  lower); Sirocco is Level 5 and excluded. Another Blizzard is excluded by its
  summon restriction. Face-up Vayu revived this way still has its material restriction.
- **Vayu → GY banish to Extra Deck summon:** Vayu 1 + Sirocco 5 = Armed Wing 6;
  Vayu + Armed Wing 6 = Armor Master 7; Vayu + Armor Master 7 = Silverwind 8.
  This is not a Synchro Summon; effects are negated and summon history matters.
  There is only one Vayu, so these are alternatives, not a free three-stage ladder.
- **Bora/Gale → hand to field:** need a differently named Blackwing you control.
  Neither searches, and Special Summoning them does not trigger Whirlwind.
- **Fortress → field materials to GY:** sideboard Cyber Dragon enables the contact
  summon with additional Machines from either field; material identity is checked.
  Goyo/Colossal provide conditional battle-based field acquisition/revival.

## Synergies

Whirlwind converts the scarce Normal Summon into another hand resource; protect
that window rather than assuming a search survives. Kalut makes Shura's battle
recruitment more credible, but taking damage into Honest is still dangerous.
Icarus converts a Winged Beast already threatened by removal into interaction;
tribute is paid before responses, and two targets must be legal at activation.
Blizzard uses spent Level 4 bodies, while Vayu removes DARKs from GY. Plan those
banishes against Dark Armed Dragon's exact-three condition and future Blizzard
options. Legacy draws are modest filtering, not a two-card draw without a Spirit.

## Combo lines

### Whirlwind + Shura + Gale in hand: Level 7 with a retained attacker

Inputs: the three named hand cards, empty/available zones, unused Normal Summon,
Bora remaining in Deck, Armor Master in Extra. No floodgate preventing summons.

1. Activate Whirlwind and allow responses. Normal Summon Shura; resolve a legal
   Whirlwind search for Bora (1700 < 1800).
2. Special Summon Bora and Gale from hand, each beside a differently named Blackwing.
3. Synchro Summon Armor Master using Gale (3) + Bora (4). Retain Shura on field.

Output: Armor Master + Shura + active Whirlwind, absent interference. Search/removal
and summon-negation windows are separate. If disruption leaves no valid Synchro,
preserve Shura/traps rather than inventing another Normal Summon. First-turn Edison
has no Battle Phase; this line does not promise an immediate attack.

### Blizzard + a Level 4 Blackwing in GY: Level 6 recovery

1. Normal Summon Blizzard with unused Normal Summon, targeting Shura or Bora in GY.
2. Resolve Defense Position revival; allow responses before proceeding.
3. Use Blizzard (2) + recovered Level 4 for Armed Wing, Brionac, or historical Goyo.

One hand card plus the specified GY setup, not a setup-free one-card combo.
A face-up Whirlwind can separately search Vayu; it cannot search Gale at equal
1300 ATK. GY banishment or negation can leave Blizzard alone; keep trap resources.

### Shura battle win → Vayu → generic Level 5

Inputs: Shura attacking an opponent monster it can destroy and send to GY, Vayu
still in Deck, available Extra Deck Catastor/Android, and a legal Main Phase 2.

1. Win the battle (Kalut requires an additional hand card if needed).
2. Resolve Shura's recruitment of Vayu, whose effects are negated.
3. In Main Phase 2 use Shura (4) + that negated Vayu (1) for Catastor or Android.

No on-field use of an unnegated Vayu is assumed. Gale recruitment instead offers
Level 7 with Shura. If battle/recruitment fails, no follow-up summon is guaranteed.

### GY Vayu + Sirocco → pressure without a Normal Summon

1. With both in GY, activate Vayu targeting Sirocco; allow GY interaction.
2. On valid resolution banish both and Special Summon Armed Wing from Extra.

Output: 2300-ATK body with negated effects; no piercing/ATK boost and no proper
Synchro summon flag. Do not revive it later as though it had been properly summoned.
Saving those DARKs can be better if Dark Armed Dragon is approaching exact-three.

### Sirocco + Bora attack concentration

Inputs: opponent controls a monster, you control none, Sirocco/Bora in hand,
unused Normal Summon and an available Battle Phase.

1. Normal Summon Sirocco without Tribute under its condition, then Special Summon Bora.
2. During Main Phase 1 target Bora with Sirocco. With only these two Blackwings on
   the entire field, Bora becomes 3700 ATK for the turn.
3. Only Bora can attack that turn; assess Kalut, Honest, set monsters and traps.

Opposing Blackwings alter the sum; removal or ATK changes alter the calculation.
Do not activate the restriction unless that single attack is better than two attacks.

## Going first and going second

First: resolve profitable Whirlwind searches, keep a Winged Beast for Icarus, and
avoid exposing multiple backrows to Heavy Storm. Dustshoot is most useful before
the opponent spends hand cards. First player draws in Edison but cannot battle.
Second: evaluate Sirocco's conditional Normal Summon, targeted removal, and steal
spells before sacrificing pressure to a reset. Compare total legal battle damage
against a Synchro line; modern Xyz/Link conversions are absent.

## Opponent-turn interaction

Use Bottomless at the proper summon window, Icarus against valuable paired targets,
Book against a threatening material/attacker, and Prison/Mirror at attack declaration.
Kalut uses the historical Damage Step window; do not treat it as arbitrary Main Phase
interaction. Solemn does not negate every Special Summon performed inside a chain.
Road requires an actual two-card destruction threat and negates its effect, not
its activation. Blind play infers no set-card identities; open coaching knows the
human state but preserves their choices and every response opportunity.

## Resource and timing ledger

Track unused Normal Summon, each Whirlwind trigger and threshold, per-instance
Gale/Sirocco/Armor Master usage, Wedge Counters, Sirocco's attack restriction,
Silverwind's Battle Phase restriction, DARK GY count, and all banish costs.
Vayu summons and Road's direct Stardust summon are not proper Synchro Summons.
Road Stardust can activate its tribute negation but cannot then revive from GY
without an earlier qualifying proper summon. Record returned control for Brain/Mind,
Stardust End Phase return eligibility, and Red Dragon's attack-history destruction.
Side Virus inspection lasts through the specified opposing turns; privately track
what was legally inspected without exposing the full hand in public narration.

## Sideboarding and matchup notes

Against Lightsworn, consider Light-Imprisoning Mirror, Crow, and Nobleman against
Ryko/Hamster; Pulling the Rug can answer qualifying Celestia Normal/Tribute Summon
triggers. Mirror also suppresses relevant LIGHT side monsters, so review your plan.
Dyna and Thunder King challenge summons/searches but conflict with your own engine;
Rai-Oh blocks Whirlwind while drawing via Allure/Legacy remains permitted.
Cyber Dragon/Fortress is specifically useful against Machine boards; Virus needs
a 2000+ ATK DARK, not an ordinary unboosted Shura. Vortex is useful against wide
face-up boards. Make equal-size swaps while retaining adequate Blackwing starters,
Winged Beasts for Icarus, and historical card-limit compliance across all sections.
No sideboard card is assumed present in Main before an agreed between-game swap.
