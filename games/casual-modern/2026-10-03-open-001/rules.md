# Casual modern rules, version 1

One game using the exact imported Branded Despia and Dracotail Main/Extra Decks.
Side Decks are snapshotted but unused. No Forbidden/Limited list is applied;
this is not a claim of tournament legality. Ordinary deck-size/copy limits apply.

Use modern TCG rules (2020 Master Rule revision), current stored English card
texts, 8000 starting LP, five-card opening hands, and the human going first.
The starting player neither draws nor enters Battle Phase on the first turn.
Each player has five Main Monster Zones, five Spell/Trap Zones, one Field Zone;
the outer Spell/Trap Zones double as Pendulum Zones. Two shared Extra Monster
Zones are tracked. Synchro/Fusion/Xyz placement uses the modern revision.

The assistant moderates, coaches the human, and plays the opponent. In open mode
it knows human hidden cards. The human sees their own hand and public board;
opponent hidden cards and future draw order are not shown. The human decides
all their actions. No automatic-pass policy is agreed. Uncertain interactions
pause at the exact decision window for authoritative rule/card-database review.
Starting-player choice was agreed in chat; independently shuffled opening hands
and deck orders are saved privately and never redrawn to improve the result.
