# Lightsworn — Edison playbook

## Overview

40 Main / 15 Extra / 15 Side. Exact list: [YDK](deck.ydk) and [gameplay data](deck.json).
Imported on 2026-10-05. This guide covers every included card; tactical lines are
conditional and preserve all opponent response opportunities. JSON stores current
YGOPRODeck text; Edison historical text/rulings take precedence as agreed in
[rules](../../../rules/edison.md). See the historical exceptions below before play.

Build a productive GY while maintaining field/hand resources; finish with
Judgment Dragon, Chaos Sorcerer, or situational Synchros. There are three Ryko and
Wulf, two Honest, Celestia, Lyla, and Judgment Dragon, but only one Lumina, Charge,
Plaguespreader, and Necro Gardna. Do not assume multiple Lumina copies or modern
Lightsworn Extra Deck support. Milling is random unless an earlier action
intentionally placed a known card on top; do not choose random mill outcomes.

## Search, summon, and recovery map

- **Charge → Deck to hand:** mill three as cost; eligible included Level 4-or-lower
  Lightsworns are Ryko, Wulf, Lyla, Aurkus, Ehren, Garoth, and Lumina. Celestia is
  Level 5, and Judgment Dragon is not a Lightsworn-named monster; neither qualifies.
- **Lumina → GY to field:** discard as cost, targeting those same eligible names
  in GY. There must already be a legal GY candidate before activation; after paying
  cost, a newly discarded eligible monster can be selected. Review Aurkus conflicts.
- **Hamster → Deck to face-down field:** Ryko is the included Level 3-or-lower Beast.
  Wulf is Beast-Warrior and therefore not a Hamster target.
- **Foolish → Deck to GY:** choose Wulf to trigger a body, Plaguespreader for a later
  Tuner, Necro Gardna for defense, or a missing Lightsworn name when appropriate.
- **Wulf → GY to field:** only sending it from Deck triggers its effect. Discarding
  Wulf from hand does not; Lumina/Call can instead Special Summon it by their effects.
- **Recharge → draw then random mill:** not a search; discard a Lightsworn as cost.
  **Card Trooper** sends random top cards as cost, not selected Deck identities.
- **Beckoning → GY to hand:** non-targeting recovery resolved after discarding your
  hand by effect. Activation requires a nonempty hand and enough LIGHT monsters
  already in GY; a responding GY removal may make it resolve without doing either.
  Judgment Dragon and Honest are valuable LIGHT recoveries.
- **Avarice → GY to Deck then draw:** exactly five eligible monsters; Extra Deck
  monsters return to Extra when appropriate. Preserve four distinct names for JD.
- **Gold Sarcophagus → Deck banish then delayed hand:** choose the needed card,
  track two subsequent own Standby Phases; not an immediate tutor.
- **Plaguespreader → GY to field:** put a hand card on top as cost. **Call** targets
  legal GY revival, while Goyo/Colossal provide battle-based acquisition/revival.
- **Fortress → field materials to GY:** side Cyber Dragon and additional Machines
  from either field; Card Trooper is a Machine, but spending it needs a benefit.

## Synergies

Recharge converts awkward Wulf hands into draws; Wulf discarded from hand does not
trigger itself. Plaguespreader can deliberately place Wulf on top before a later
mill, provided no draw/shuffle/order change intervenes. Garoth benefits from another
controlled Lightsworn monster's mill effect, not Charge/Recharge, Card Trooper,
Celestia's milling cost, or Judgment Dragon (its name is not Lightsworn).
Ryko/Hamster buys time and loads the GY; Honest turns LIGHT combat into profitable
trades. JD wants different names, not four copies; Chaos Sorcerer/Avarice can remove
those names and break that condition. Gorz requires an empty field, including backrow;
Tragoedia does not, but shrinking the hand shrinks its stats. Aurkus protection is
symmetric and can interfere with your own targeted Lightsworn effects.

## Combo lines

### Foolish Burial → Wulf: targeted body setup

Inputs: Foolish in hand, Wulf remaining in Deck, free monster zone, no blocking
summon effect. Send Wulf from Deck to GY; after that chain ends, place Wulf's trigger
in its correct window and Special Summon on resolution. Output is a Level 4/2100
body, without spending a Normal Summon. GY removal or effect negation can stop it.
An independent tribute/Tuner card is required for Celestia/Synchro follow-up.

### Lumina converts a discarded Wulf into a real field body

Inputs: Lumina and Wulf in hand, unused Normal Summon, and an eligible Lightsworn
already in GY (for example Ryko), free zones, no active Aurkus targeting conflict.

1. Normal Summon Lumina and allow responses.
2. Activate its revival effect; discard Wulf as cost and select that Wulf now in GY.
3. Resolve revival. Wulf did not trigger from discard; Lumina supplies the summon.

Output: Lumina + Wulf, with a pending End Phase mill from Lumina if applicable.
Without the preexisting eligible GY monster activation is illegal. If the target
is banished, do not choose a replacement at resolution unless a rule permits it.

### Lumina + GY Lyla/Plaguespreader + two payment cards → Mist Wurm

1. With an unused Normal Summon, Normal Summon Lumina; discard one payment card
   to revive Lyla already in GY, allowing responses.
2. Put the other payment hand card on top to revive Plaguespreader from GY.
3. Synchro Summon Mist Wurm with Plaguespreader (2), Lumina (3), and Lyla (4).
4. Resolve its targeted bounce on up to three opposing field cards.

Inputs include both GY resources and two hand payments in addition to Lumina; not
one-card or guaranteed. Output: Mist Wurm, used Lightsworns in GY, and banished
self-revived Plaguespreader. Keep remaining hand/deck and interruption costs in view.
If a summon is stopped, preserve useful LIGHT bodies rather than forcing a line.

### Plaguespreader + Lyla → Tempest; ordinary Level 6 alternatives

Inputs: Lyla already face-up, Plaguespreader in GY, hand card for its revival cost.
Revive Plaguespreader; 2 + 4 makes Tempest Magician with a Spellcaster non-Tuner,
or generic historical Goyo, Brionac, or Gaia Knight. Tempest gets its summon counter;
burning it alone is 500 damage, not an infinite combo. Further counters require
actual discard resources. Arcanite is Level 7 and cannot be made from this pair.
Arcanite needs an appropriate acquired opposing Spellcaster/material arrangement;
its presence in Extra is not proof of a routine own-card line.

### Celestia tribute removal

Inputs: Celestia in hand, a controlled Lightsworn, unused Normal Summon, at least
four Deck cards for the historical cost, and one or two legal opposing field targets.

1. Tribute the Lightsworn to Tribute Summon Celestia, preserving summon responses.
2. Activate its optional trigger, send four top Deck cards as cost, and target one
   or two opposing cards under historical rulings.
3. Resolve destruction and then handle any pending Wulf/mill-related triggers.

Tributing a non-Lightsworn does not enable removal. One target avoids the specific
Starlight Road two-card requirement; two targets may gain more but invite it.
Garoth does not trigger from the cost mill. Negating Celestia does not refund costs.

### Judgment Dragon commitment, optionally protected by Cold Wave

Inputs: JD in hand, four distinct Lightsworn names currently in GY, more than
1000 LP if you intend to pay and survive, legal summon availability, sufficient
follow-up resources. Cold Wave is an additional hand card, not assumed available.

1. If using Cold Wave, activate it at the start of Main Phase 1 before any other
   Main Phase action. Let responses resolve; it also restricts your own Spell/Traps.
2. Recheck four names at the actual summon point. Special Summon JD by its procedure.
3. Apply the agreed ignition-priority rules after a successful summon; declare JD's
   destruction effect and pay 1000, then allow legal monster/other responses.
4. Resolve the wipe; assess remaining hand effects and legal battle damage. Do not
   assume Gorz/Tragoedia or monster-based disruption was disabled by Cold Wave.

Output depends on responses and hand follow-up. No Battle Phase on the first turn
and no guaranteed lethal. JD destroys your own other cards too; its own End Phase
mill remains relevant. Without Cold Wave, Road and destruction protection matter.

## Going first and going second

First: prefer productive Ryko/Hamster sets or an engine turn with defensible follow-up;
track compulsory End Phase mills and the starting-player draw. Do not overextend
just to mill or spend JD names on low-value Synchros. Second: assess Lyla/backrow
removal, Celestia, stolen material through Brain Control, and a JD commit. Decide
whether to use Cold Wave before taking any other Main Phase 1 action. Honest only
supports LIGHT attackers; Catastor is poor against a DARK Blackwing board.

## Opponent-turn interaction

Necro Gardna can protect against the next attack but not freely choose which future
attack is negated after activation. Honest follows the historical Damage Step timing.
Gorz demands no controlled cards, whereas Tragoedia triggers after battle damage
without that restriction. Book and Call have separate response/target windows;
Beckoning needs legal hand/GY counts. Sided Solemn, Bottomless, Mirror, Torrential,
Dust Tornado, and My Body operate only once actually placed into Main and set/usable.
My Body costs 1500 and covers qualifying destruction, not arbitrary negation.

## Resource and timing ledger

Maintain a set of distinct Lightsworn names in GY, LIGHT/DARK banish supplies,
remaining Deck count, each physical card's turn usage, and mandatory End Phase
obligations. JD is not a Lightsworn name for Charge, Recharge, Garoth, or its own
four-name requirement. Resolve each mill separately; establish triggered Wulf and
Garoth chains legally, not while another chain is still resolving. Do not lose
immediately merely for milling the last card; a failed required draw is different.
Track Plaguespreader's banish-on-leaving flag, deliberate top-deck placement,
Lyla's position lock, Chaos Sorcerer's attack restriction, Tragoedia's copied Level
and changing ATK, Spell Counters, Cold Wave expiry, Sarcophagus return dates, Call
links, and Gorz Token's damage-derived ATK/DEF. Explicitly create that Token in the
state; do not invent a normal deck card for it. Properly summoned bosses and
Road-type summons elsewhere in the game need different GY revival eligibility.

## Sideboarding and matchup notes

Against Blackwing, consider Consecrated Light, Bottomless, and Dust Tornado for
Whirlwind/Mirror or other revealed backrow. Consecrated Light blocks your own
Plaguespreader, Chaos Sorcerer, Gorz, Tragoedia, and DARK Synchros from being summoned;
plan corresponding swaps and do not try to ignore that symmetric restriction.
Kycoo can deny opposing Vayu/GY banish costs and threaten DARK counts through battle.
Cyber/Fortress targets Machine matchups. Giant Trunade and My Body can protect
commitments against relevant backrow; Mirror/Torrential are conditional resets.
Preserve equal Main/Side sizes, enough Lightsworn names and Recharge fodder, useful
GY DARKs for Sorcerer, and historical limits across all sections. Exact swaps
require the observed matchup and first/second choice, not invented opponent hands.
