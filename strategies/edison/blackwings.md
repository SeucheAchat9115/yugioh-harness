# Blackwings: game plan

General advice; adapt it to the cards in the selected decklist.

- Black Whirlwind turns Normal Summons into repeated searches.
  The summoned monster's ATK determines which Blackwings can be searched.
- After destroying a monster by battle, Shura can summon a Blackwing with
  1500 ATK or less from the Deck; that monster's effects are negated.
- Bora and Gale enable quick field development alongside other Blackwings.
- Kalut influences damage calculations. Handle its activation in the Damage
  Step and any possible responses according to the historical rules.
- Icarus Attack can turn one Winged Beast into two destroyed cards.
  Declare the tribute and targets when activating it.

Against Lightsworn: watch for Lumina and Ryko, preserve resources for later
turns, and expect Judgment Dragon when the opponent's Graveyard contains four
different Lightsworn names. Wulf and Necro Gardna affect how safe an attack
or apparent field advantage really is.
