# Lightsworn: game plan

General advice; adapt it to the cards in the selected decklist.

- Mill cards to place resources and different Lightsworn names in the Graveyard.
- Judgment Dragon requires at least four different Lightsworn names in your
  Graveyard. Its destruction effect costs 1000 LP.
- Lumina turns a card in hand into a Level 4 or lower Lightsworn monster from
  the Graveyard; declare the cost and target before resolution.
- When Wulf is sent from the Deck to the Graveyard by a card effect, it can
  summon itself; drawing it into your hand makes it harder to use.
- Charge of the Light Brigade sends the top three cards to the Graveyard as
  its cost. Solar Recharge discards a Lightsworn as its cost, draws two cards,
  and mills two cards.
- Resolve End Phase effects individually and keep track of the remaining Deck size.

Against Blackwings: account for Kalut before attacking, watch for Icarus Attack
when facing set cards, and answer Black Whirlwind early if possible.
Judgment Dragon can clear strong fields, but follow-up actions need protection.
